## Intro

I can't believe it. I never would have imagined it.

I got to Perth a year ago. Three months later, The Pixies decided to perform. I went to see them live. Holding a can of beer in my hand. Knowing all the lyrics by heart. Enjoying the show.

Now it's happening with Radiohead.

The last time they were here was 15 years ago.

I heard them for the first time when I was 15 years old.

And now they're coming to Perth.

📻 🙂

## A week ago

A week ago, Radiohead announced:
> Radiohead return to Australia for the first time in fifteen years, playing sixteen shows across Perth, Melbourne + Sydney in May-Jun 2027.

It all looked like a quest to me.

A radio quest.
